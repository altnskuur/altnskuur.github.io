Biography

I am a Software Engineer currently working at Meva Avionics (Baykar's avionics subsidiary), where I design and develop low-level firmware and embedded Linux solutions for mission-critical aerospace and defense systems. My work bridges the gap between raw hardware registers and high-performance operating systems, focusing on robust system architecture, hardware abstraction, and reliable device communication.

Recently, I have been heavily focused on architecting and deploying a high-performance embedded Linux NAS system. This involved implementing a comprehensive RAID-based storage architecture for fault-tolerant data management, tuning Linux kernel parameters via sysctl to optimize resource utilization (CPU, RAM, I/O), and benchmarking Samba/NFS services over high-speed Ethernet. Alongside this, I have worked extensively on hardware validation layers, QSPI/I2C/CAN protocols, and GNSS/GSM module integration using AT commands.

My engineering journey traces back to TOBB University of Economics and Technology (TOBB ETÜ), where I graduated with a B.Sc. in Electrical and Electronics Engineering. Beyond the rigorous coursework, my university years were heavily defined by active leadership and community involvement. I served as the President of the IEEE Student Branch and led the Gastronomy Club, combining technical organization with cultural and social events. Alongside leading our team as Team Captain for ETU Callisto in the TEKNOFEST Model Satellite Competition, these experiences taught me how to manage multidisciplinary teams, bridge diverse interests, and drive collaborative projects from scratch.

Outside of the embedded world, I try to keep a balance between staying active and exploring new cultures. I am a certified 1-star CMAS diver, a former licensed basketball player, and you can regularly find me playing pickup football every Tuesday evening or hitting the gym 2–3 times a week. I love discovering different cultures and history—so far exploring cities like Berlin, Budapest, Samos, Bratislava, and Vienna—while currently grinding to earn my TOEFL certification to add alongside my German TELC A2 certificate.
